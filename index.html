Our story

Started in a kitchen
that seated six.

Camey started baking sourdough for neighbors in 2014, delivering warm loaves by bicycle before her day job. What began as a weekend hobby outgrew her kitchen within a year — by 2016 she’d opened the doors on Maple Street with her parents mixing dough before dawn and her kids boxing cookies after school.

Every recipe on our shelves is still one of ours: a grandmother’s cinnamon roll, a father’s rye starter, a decade of birthday cakes refined one candle at a time. We bake in small batches, which means some days we sell out early — that’s why we built pre-ordering, so your favorite is waiting even on our busiest mornings.
